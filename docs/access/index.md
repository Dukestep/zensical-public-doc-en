# Access

The Meteorological Service of Canada (MSC) offers several ways to access weather, water, ice and climate data and services. Choose the access method that best suits your needs:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-api: **MSC GeoMet**

    ---

    Interoperable web services and APIs (WMS, OGC API, WCS, STAC) for on-demand access to real-time and archived weather, climate and water data.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: More info](../msc-geomet/index.md)

-   :material-server: **MSC Datamart**

    ---

    A raw HTTPS data server for automated, high-volume retrieval of weather, water, climate and environmental data, with real-time push notifications via AMQP.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: More info](../msc-datamart/index.md)

-   :material-briefcase: **Cost-recovered services**

    ---

    Dedicated data feeds, archived data retrieval and other specialized services available under a cost-recovery model for needs beyond the free open data offering.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: More info](../cost-recovered/index.md)

-   :lucide-layers-plus: **MSC AniMet**

    ---

    A web-based tool to visualize, combine and animate MSC weather, climate and water layers, and share the results via permalink.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: More info](../msc-animet/index.md)

-   :material-earth: **WMO WIS2**

    ---

    Canada's contribution to the WMO Information System 2.0, a global framework for discovering and accessing international weather, water and climate data via open standards.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: More info](../wmo-wis2/index.md)

</div>
